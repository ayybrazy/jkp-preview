How it works

What actually happens when you reach out.

A plain-terms walkthrough: what John does, what he doesn't do, and what to expect at each step. If something below doesn't answer your question, call or text him directly.

What happens when you reach out.

  1. 1

    A short conversation

    You tell John where you are and what you want to happen. No pressure, no commitment.

  2. 2

    He makes the introductions

    Once you say go, John connects you with a lender and Realtor from his network who work with VA buyers.

  3. 3

    He stays in it

    John remains a point of contact through closing, so you always have someone to ask.

John isn't the lender and doesn't decide eligibility. He connects VA buyers with VA-experienced professionals nationwide. Selling and home valuations in Central Ohio are handled by John directly.

Common questions.

What if I'm not ready, or not sure I qualify?

That's the normal starting point. You don't need to be approved or certain of anything. John helps you understand where you stand and what a realistic next step looks like.

What is John's role, versus the lender and the agent?

John isn't the lender and doesn't decide eligibility. He connects VA buyers with VA-experienced professionals nationwide. Selling and home valuations in Central Ohio are handled by John directly.

What if I want to use a VA loan?

John helps you understand how a VA loan works for a home purchase, then connects you with a lender and Realtor from his network who work with VA buyers. He isn't the lender and doesn't decide eligibility.

What if I want to sell, or just want to know what my home is worth?

John provides direct Realtor service for selling and home valuations in Central Ohio.
