Privacy Policy

John Kalo Properties LLC · Effective November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how John Kalo Properties LLC ("John Kalo Properties," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, fill out our forms, or receive text messages from us. John Kalo is a real estate salesperson with eXp Realty.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out a form on our website, we collect:

  • Your first and last name, mobile phone number, and email address
  • Whether you want to buy or sell a home, and whether you prefer a call or a text
  • Any optional details you choose to add, such as the areas you're interested in, your property address, your timeline, or your budget
  • Your agreement to be contacted by call or text message, and the date and time you submitted the form

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we collect the page you landed on, the website that referred you, and campaign details included in the link you clicked (such as which ad or post it came from). Our website uses analytics and advertising cookies, including Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel, to measure visits and form submissions. We do not send your name, email address, or phone number to Google or Meta.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and help you buy or sell a home
  • To contact you by phone, email, or text message, based on your preferences and consent
  • To send you a one-time code that verifies your phone number, when you request one
  • To keep a record of your inquiry in our customer relationship management system
  • To understand which pages and ads are working, so we can improve our website and marketing
  • To prevent spam, fraud, and abuse of our forms
  • To comply with the law and with real estate brokerage requirements

Text messaging (SMS)

The only automated text message we send is the verification code you request. You agree to be contacted by call or text message by checking the consent box on our form. The box is never checked for you. It must be checked to continue, because the form asks us to contact you. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of buying or selling a home or of any other service.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party, except the service providers that deliver our text messages for us.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages, or HELP for help. See the SMS Terms in our Terms & Conditions.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as our customer relationship management system, our text messaging and phone verification provider, our form processing tools, and our website host. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • With eXp Realty, the brokerage John Kalo is licensed with, when needed to provide real estate services or meet brokerage and legal requirements, never for marketing
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our clients, or the public

Cookies and your choices

Our website also saves your light or dark display choice in your browser, so pages look the way you left them. That setting stays on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can block or delete cookies and saved site data in your browser settings. Our website still works without them. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. To unsubscribe from email, use the link in any email or contact us. To ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, contact us using the details below.

How long we keep information

We keep your information as long as we need it to respond to your inquiry, provide real estate services, keep business records, and meet legal and brokerage requirements.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we'll change the effective date at the top of this page.
