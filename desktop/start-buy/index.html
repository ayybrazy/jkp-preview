Questions people ask first.

Do I need to be pre-approved before I talk to John?

No. Most people reach out before they talk to a lender. John helps you understand where you stand, then connects you with a lender when you're ready.

How does buying with a VA loan work?

John walks you through the process and connects you with a lender and Realtor who work with VA buyers. He isn't the lender and doesn't decide eligibility.

What if I'm buying outside Central Ohio?

John connects VA buyers with VA-experienced professionals nationwide and stays a point of contact through closing.

What is my home worth?

John looks at your home and recent sales nearby and gives you a straight answer. Home valuations in Central Ohio are handled by John directly.

Is now a good time to sell?

It depends on your home, your timing, and what you need from the sale. That's the first conversation, and there's no commitment.

What do I need to do before listing?

Nothing yet. Tell John about the house and he will tell you what is worth doing and what is not.
